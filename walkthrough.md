## 📸 What's New:

1. **Polaroid Memory Gallery Animation 🖼️**:
   - As soon as the gift explodes with light, the **"Our Sweet Memories Together"** polaroid cards rise smoothly from the bottom with a burst of stars and hearts.
   - Styled as glossy polaroids with golden borders, playful tilting angles, and sweet romantic captions:
     - **Photo 1 (`1.jpg`)**: *"Forever With You 💖 — Always you ❤️"*
     - **Photo 2 (`2.jpg`)**: *"7 Years Strong 🎄 — My favorite smile ✨"*
     - **Photo 3 (`3.jpg`)**: *"Almost 8 Years ❤️ — My favorite gift 🎁"*

2. **Interactive Micro-Interactions ✨**:
   - Hovering/tapping on any memory card straightens it, zooms gently, and reveals a dark romantic gradient overlay with caption.
   - Clicking any photo produces a burst of colorful heart/star particles with a gentle chime.

3. **Smooth & Lag-Free Integration ⚡**:
   - Hardware-accelerated transitions ensure that the photos load and transition in 60 FPS without any lag.
